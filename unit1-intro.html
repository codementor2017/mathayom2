📖 Welcome to Your Grade 8 Textbook

Welcome to your Grade 8 Technology and Computing Science textbook. In this book, you will learn how to think like a computer scientist.

You will discover how to:

  • Break complex problems into smaller parts.
  • Find patterns that make problems easier.
  • Create simple models of complicated situations.
  • Design step-by-step solutions that anyone can follow.

Every chapter builds on the one before it. By the end of Unit 1, you will be able to take a real-life problem, analyze it thoroughly, and design an efficient solution using computational thinking.
