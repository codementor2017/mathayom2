7.1 How Do Devices Connect? ⏱️ 0–10 min

How does your phone connect to the internet?

Wi-Fi, mobile data, cable, satellite.

What is a network?

A network is two or more computers connected to share data and resources.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

How many devices at home or at school are connected to the same network?

7.2 Types of Networks ⏱️ 10–20 min

🏫 LAN (Local Area Network)

A small area (home, school, office).

Example: School Wi-Fi.

🌆 WAN (Wide Area Network)

A large area (city, country, world).

Example: Bank branches connected across a country.

🌐 Internet

The largest network — a global network of networks.

7.3 Network Devices ⏱️ 20–30 min

  • Router: Connects networks and directs traffic.
  • Switch: Connects devices within a LAN.
  • Modem: Connects your home to the internet service provider.
  • Access point: Provides Wi-Fi.

Every device on a network has a unique IP address, like a house address.

A simple network diagram:

Internet → Modem → Router → Switch → Devices (computers, phones, printers)

☕ Break Time ⏱️ 50–60 min

Stretch, hydrate, and reset your brain. When you return, you'll design a school network! 🦁

7.5 Group Activity: Design a School Network ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work in a group. Design a network for a school with 3 buildings, 20 classrooms, 5 offices, and 1 library.

  • Identify where routers, switches, and access points go.
  • Decide where Wi-Fi is needed.
  • Draw a network diagram.
  • Explain how data travels from a student's laptop to the internet.

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Planner: decides where each device goes.
  • Illustrator: draws the network diagram.
  • Analyst: explains the data path.
  • Presenter: presents to the class.

7.5b Sharing & Presentations ⏱️ 85–95 min

Each group presents their network design.

🗣️ Peer Feedback:

Give one star and one wish.

7.6 Chapter Summary ⏱️ 95–97 min

  • A network is two or more computers connected to share data.
  • LAN is a small area. WAN is a large area. The internet is the largest network.
  • Routers, switches, modems, and access points connect devices.
  • Every device has a unique IP address.
