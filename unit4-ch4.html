4.1 Your Digital Shadow ⏱️ 0–10 min

If someone searched your name online, what would they find?

Maybe photos, comments, tagged posts, public records, or social media profiles.

Your digital footprint is like a shadow. It follows you everywhere, even when you are not looking.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

If a teacher or future employer searched your name, what would they find? Would you be proud?

4.2 Passive and Active Footprints ⏱️ 10–20 min

There are two types of digital footprint:

👣 Passive

Things others post about you, tagged photos, comments.

👣 Active

Things you choose to post, share, or create.

You cannot fully control your passive footprint, but you can control your active footprint.

4.3 Create, Don't Just Consume ⏱️ 20–30 min

Instead of posting random content, build something valuable.

Examples:

  • A portfolio of your work.
  • A blog about a hobby.
  • A YouTube channel with tutorials.
  • A GitHub with code projects.
  • A photography account.
  • A podcast about a topic you care about.

A purposeful digital footprint can help you in the future. Universities and employers check.

4.4 The 3-Step Management Strategy ⏱️ 30–40 min

🔍 Audit

Search your name and check what appears.

🧹 Clean

Delete or archive content that does not represent you well.

🌱 Build

Create content that reflects your goals and values.

👩‍🏫 Tip: Encourage students to actually search their own name once during the lesson. It can be a powerful moment of self-awareness.

4.5 Audit a Sample Footprint ⏱️ 40–50 min

Imagine a fictional student named Sam. Here is what appears when you search Sam's name:

  • Posts from 3 years ago with bad language.
  • Tagged photos at parties.
  • Comments arguing with strangers.
  • A blog about gaming.
  • A YouTube channel with tutorials.

✅ Positive elements

The blog and YouTube channel show Sam's interests and skills.

❌ Negative elements

Bad language, party photos, and arguments reflect poorly on Sam.

🧹 What should Sam do?

  • Delete or archive the old posts with bad language.
  • Untag or remove the party photos.
  • Stop arguing with strangers.
  • Continue building the blog and YouTube channel.

🌱 What should Sam build?

  • A professional portfolio.
  • More tutorials.
  • A LinkedIn profile.
🗣️ Quick Check (2 minutes):

What advice would you give Sam? Why?

☕ Break Time ⏱️ 50–60 min

Stretch, hydrate, and reset your brain. When you return, you'll design your own digital presence plan! 🦁

4.6 Group Activity: Design Your Digital Presence Plan ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work with a partner. Create a plan for your own digital presence:

  1. What kind of content do I want to be known for?
  2. What should I delete or archive?
  3. What should I build?
  4. What privacy settings should I use?
  5. What is my 1-year goal?

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Auditor: helps identify current content.
  • Planner: writes the plan.
  • Designer: draws a visual roadmap.
  • Presenter: explains the plan to the class.

4.6b Sharing & Presentations ⏱️ 85–95 min

Each pair presents their digital presence plan.

🗣️ Peer Feedback:

Give one star and one wish.

4.7 Chapter Summary ⏱️ 95–97 min

  • A digital footprint is the trail of data you leave online.
  • Passive footprint is what others post. Active footprint is what you post.
  • Create valuable content instead of random posts.
  • The 3-step strategy: Audit, Clean, Build.
  • A purposeful footprint can help you in the future.
