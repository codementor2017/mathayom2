8.1 Have You Ever Reported? ⏱️ 0–10 min

Have you ever reported a post or account? What happened?

Reporting is an important tool for keeping platforms safe.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

Why do you think some people don't report harmful content? Share with your partner.

8.2 Platform Tools ⏱️ 10–20 min

Most platforms have these tools:

🚨 Report

Flag content for review.

🚫 Block

Prevent someone from contacting you.

🔇 Mute

Stop seeing someone's content without blocking them.

🔒 Restrict

Limit someone's interaction with you.

⚙️ Privacy settings

Control who sees your content.

8.3 Escalation ⏱️ 20–30 min

If the platform does not respond, you can escalate:

  1. Step 1: Use the platform's report tool.
  2. Step 2: If nothing happens, tell a trusted adult.
  3. Step 3: If the issue is serious, contact the school, parent, or authority.
  4. Step 4: For legal issues, contact the police or a lawyer.
👩‍🏫 Tip: Emphasize that escalation is not "tattling" — it's a responsible step when the platform doesn't act. Serious cases need the right authority.

8.4 How to Write a Good Report ⏱️ 30–50 min

A good report answers these questions:

  • What happened?
  • When did it happen?
  • Who was involved?
  • What evidence do you have?
  • What did you already try?

Here is an example:

What happened: An anonymous account sends mean messages to my classmate daily.
When: Started 3 days ago.
Who: Unknown account, but affecting a classmate.
Evidence: Screenshots, message history.
What was tried: Told the classmate to block the account.
🗣️ Quick Check (2 minutes):

Why is it important to include evidence in your report?

☕ Break Time ⏱️ 50–60 min

Stretch, hydrate, and reset your brain. When you return, you'll role-play reporting! 🦁

8.5 Group Activity: Role-Play Reporting ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work with a partner. Choose a scenario.

  • An anonymous account sending mean messages.
  • A fake account impersonating a teacher.
  • A scam link shared in a group chat.
  • A harmful post targeting a classmate.

Then:

  1. Identify the correct platform tools to use.
  2. Write a report using the five questions.
  3. Role-play contacting a trusted adult.

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Reporter: writes and reads the report.
  • Trusted Adult: listens and responds.
  • Observer: checks that all five questions are covered.
  • Presenter: summarizes the role-play for the class.

After each role-play, give feedback:

  • Did they identify the right tools?
  • Did they write a clear report?
  • Did they escalate appropriately?

8.5b Sharing & Performances ⏱️ 85–95 min

Each pair performs their role-play.

🗣️ Peer Feedback:

Give one star and one wish.

8.6 Chapter Summary ⏱️ 95–97 min

  • Platform tools include report, block, mute, restrict, and privacy settings.
  • Escalation means moving the issue to a higher authority.
  • A good report answers: What, when, who, evidence, and what was tried.
  • Reporting is an important part of being a responsible digital citizen.
