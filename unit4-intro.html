📖 Welcome to Unit 4

In Unit 1, you learned how to think like a computer scientist. In Unit 2, you learned how to use logic and functions in programming. In Unit 3, you learned how computer systems and communication technology work.

Now it is time to think about something just as important: how to be a responsible digital citizen.

In Grade 7, you learned how to protect yourself — privacy, passwords, scams, and the basics of Creative Commons. In Grade 8, you will go further. You will learn how to respond to harm, take responsibility for what you publish, own your work, license it, and protect it.

You are not just a consumer of technology. You are a creator. You have rights. You have responsibilities. And you have the power to make the online world better.

🗣️ Quick Warm-Up (3 minutes):

What does "being responsible online" mean to you? Share with a partner.

🎯 What You Will Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

  • Respond to inappropriate content appropriately.
  • Take responsibility for what you publish.
  • Own your work and license it.
  • Protect your digital creations.
  • Build a purposeful digital presence.
