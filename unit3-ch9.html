9.1 Wireless in Daily Life ⏱️ 0–10 min

What devices do you use that are wireless?

Phones, earbuds, smartwatch, game controller, TV remote, Wi-Fi.

How do they communicate without wires?

Through radio waves, infrared, or other wireless signals.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

How many wireless devices do you think are in your home right now? Make a quick guess with your partner.

9.2 Types of Wireless Technology ⏱️ 10–30 min

📶 Wi-Fi

Connects devices to a local network and the internet.

Range: 30–100 m. Speed: High. Used for: internet access.

🎧 Bluetooth

Connects devices over short distances.

Range: ~10 m. Speed: Low to medium. Used for: earbuds, speakers, file sharing.

📱 Mobile networks (3G, 4G, 5G)

Connects phones to the internet over long distances.

Range: Kilometers. Speed: High (5G very high). Used for: calls, data, streaming.

💳 NFC (Near Field Communication)

Very short range (~4 cm).

Used for: contactless payments, transit cards.

📦 RFID (Radio Frequency Identification)

Used for: tracking items, access cards, inventory.

9.5 Group Activity: Wireless Technology Investigation ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work in a group. Your teacher will assign your group a wireless technology: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 4G/5G, NFC, or RFID.

Research and present:

  1. How it works (simple explanation).
  2. Range and speed.
  3. Common uses.
  4. Advantages and disadvantages.
  5. One interesting fact.

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Researcher: finds facts about the technology.
  • Writer: organizes the presentation.
  • Illustrator: draws the signal diagram.
  • Presenter: delivers the presentation.

9.5b Sharing & Presentations ⏱️ 85–95 min

Each group presents their wireless technology.

🗣️ Peer Feedback:

Give one star and one wish.

9.6 Chapter Summary ⏱️ 95–97 min

  • Wireless technology sends data without wires.
  • The main types are Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile networks, NFC, and RFID.
  • Each has different ranges, speeds, and uses.
  • Choose the right technology for the task.
