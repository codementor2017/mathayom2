📘 What You Learned ⏱️ 0–10 min

In this unit, you learned:

  • The difference between safe and responsible IT use.
  • The four responsibilities of a digital citizen.
  • How to respond to inappropriate content.
  • The impact of publishing and how to think before you post.
  • How to manage your digital footprint.
  • What copyright is and how to assert ownership.
  • How to choose and apply a Creative Commons license.
  • How to protect your digital creations.
  • How to report and escalate issues.
  • How to build a responsible digital presence.
  • How to create, license, and protect a digital work.

🧠 Concept Check ⏱️ 30–50 min

Part A: Apply the four responsibilities to this scenario.

"A classmate shares a violent video in your class group chat."

📢 Respond

Report it to a trusted adult or the platform.

🛑 Prevent

Leave the group or mute it. Block the sender if needed.

🚫 Do not retaliate

Don't post angry messages or share more harmful content.

📵 Do not spread

Don't forward, screenshot, or repost the video.

Part B: Apply a license to your own work.

Choose a piece of work you've created. Decide:

  • What do you want to allow?
  • What do you want to restrict?
  • Which license fits?

Write a license statement:

"This work is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0."
🗣️ Class Discussion (5 minutes):

Why do you think it's important for creators to choose a license, instead of leaving it unclear?
