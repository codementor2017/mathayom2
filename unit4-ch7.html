7.1 Someone Stole My Work ⏱️ 0–10 min

Imagine a student posts a photo. A week later, someone else posts the same photo without credit and claims it as their own.

What can the original creator do?

They can use protection tools, gather evidence, and respond.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

If this happened to you, what would you do first? Share with your partner.

7.2 Tools for Protection ⏱️ 10–30 min

💧 Watermark

A visible mark on your image or video (like your name or logo).

📝 Metadata

Invisible information embedded in a file (creator, date, camera).

⏰ Timestamp

Proves when you created or published something.

📄 Proof of ownership

Original files, drafts, behind-the-scenes content.

📚 Platform upload history

Your post history proves you posted first.

🔒 Password protection

For documents and files.

7.3 How to Respond to Unauthorized Use ⏱️ 30–40 min

If someone uses your work without permission:

  1. Contact the person politely and ask them to credit you or take it down.
  2. Use the platform's report function.
  3. Send a formal takedown request.
  4. For serious cases, consult an adult or legal authority.
👩‍🏫 Tip: Emphasize that the first step should be polite. Many cases are honest mistakes, and a friendly message often resolves them quickly.

7.4 Analyze Protection Methods ⏱️ 40–50 min

Read each work. What protection method would be best?

Work
Best Protection
A digital illustration
Watermark, metadata, timestamp
A blog post
Timestamp, proof of drafts, platform history
A song recording
Metadata, copyright notice, platform history
A short film
Watermark, metadata, credits
A piece of software code
License file, version control (like Git), timestamp
🗣️ Quick Check (2 minutes):

Which protection method do you think is the most important? Why?

☕ Break Time ⏱️ 50–60 min

Stretch, hydrate, and reset your brain. When you return, you'll create your own protection plan! 🦁

7.5 Group Activity: Create a Protection Plan ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work with a partner. Choose a type of digital work. Create a protection plan:

  1. How will you mark ownership?
  2. How will you prove ownership?
  3. How will you detect unauthorized use?
  4. How will you respond if it happens?

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Creator: describes the work.
  • Protector: designs the protection plan.
  • Writer: documents the plan.
  • Presenter: explains the plan to the class.

7.5b Sharing & Presentations ⏱️ 85–95 min

Each pair presents their protection plan.

🗣️ Peer Feedback:

Give one star and one wish.

7.6 Chapter Summary ⏱️ 95–97 min

  • Digital creations can be copied or stolen.
  • Protection tools include watermarks, metadata, timestamps, and proof of ownership.
  • You can respond to unauthorized use by contacting the person, reporting, or sending a takedown request.
  • The best protection is a combination of tools.
