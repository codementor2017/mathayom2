9.1 Who Do You Admire Online? ⏱️ 0–10 min

Who is a content creator you admire? Why?

What makes them responsible?

Maybe they credit their sources. Maybe they fact-check before posting. Maybe they respond to comments respectfully.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

Name one creator you admire. What makes them a good role model online?

9.2 Three Principles of Responsible Creation ⏱️ 10–30 min

🤝 Respect

Respect others' privacy, rights, and dignity.

🛡️ Responsibility

Think about the impact of what you publish.

⚖️ Rights

Understand and assert your own rights as a creator.

A responsible creator builds content that adds value, not harm.

9.3 Examples ⏱️ 30–40 min

🌟 Responsible creators:

  • A photographer who credits their subjects.
  • A vlogger who fact-checks before posting.
  • A designer who licenses their work openly.
  • A writer who corrects mistakes publicly.

⚠️ Irresponsible creators:

  • Someone who posts without fact-checking.
  • Someone who uses others' work without credit.
  • Someone who spreads harmful content.

9.4 Analyze Creator Scenarios ⏱️ 40–50 min

Read each scenario. Is this responsible? Why or why not?

🗣️ Quick Check (2 minutes):

What is one thing all of these irresponsible creators have in common?

☕ Break Time ⏱️ 50–60 min

Stretch, hydrate, and reset your brain. When you return, you'll design your own Digital Creator Charter! 🦁

9.5 Group Activity: Design Your Digital Creator Charter ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work with a partner. Answer these questions:

  1. What kind of content do I want to create?
  2. What values guide my content?
  3. How will I respect others?
  4. How will I take responsibility?
  5. How will I assert my rights?
  6. What license will I use?

Create a "Digital Creator Charter" — a short document that summarizes your principles as a creator.

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Creator: describes the kind of content.
  • Philosopher: identifies the values and principles.
  • Writer: writes the charter.
  • Presenter: reads and explains the charter.

9.5b Sharing & Presentations ⏱️ 85–95 min

Each pair shares their Digital Creator Charter.

🗣️ Peer Feedback:

Give one star and one wish.

9.6 Chapter Summary ⏱️ 95–97 min

  • The three principles are respect, responsibility, and rights.
  • A responsible creator adds value, not harm.
  • You can design your digital presence intentionally.
  • A Digital Creator Charter helps you stay true to your values.
