10.1 Project Overview ⏱️ 0–10 min

Now it is time to use everything you have learned. You will design a complete system that uses hardware, software, and communication technology to solve a real problem.

Your project should include:

  • A clear problem statement.
  • Hardware components identified.
  • Software components identified.
  • Communication technology chosen and justified.
  • A data flow diagram.
  • A clear explanation of how the system solves the problem.
  • A clear presentation.
🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

Which system from Unit 3 was the most interesting to you? Why?

10.2 Project Options ⏱️ 10–20 min

Choose one:

  • A smart school attendance system.
  • A home security system with cameras and alerts.
  • A system for a small business to manage orders and communicate with customers.
  • A health monitoring system for elderly people.
  • A smart farming system that monitors soil and weather.
  • A school emergency notification system.
  • A library book tracking system.
  • A traffic management system for a busy intersection.
👩‍🏫 Tip: Encourage students to choose a system that matters to them or their community. Personal relevance improves engagement.

10.3 Planning Your Project ⏱️ 20–50 min

Write down:

📝 Problem statement

What problem are you solving? Why does it matter?

🖥️ Hardware needed

List the physical devices (computers, sensors, cameras, etc.).

📀 Software needed

List the applications, databases, or apps.

📡 Communication technology

Choose Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 4G/5G, NFC, or RFID — and justify.

🔄 Data flow

Map: input → process → output → storage.

✅ How it solves the problem

Explain the benefits and limitations.

Check with your teacher before you continue.

☕ Break Time ⏱️ 50–60 min

Stretch, hydrate, and reset your brain. When you return, you'll create your project poster! 🦁

10.4 Designing Your Project ⏱️ 60–85 min

Create your project:

  • Poster, slides, or diagram.
  • Include hardware, software, and communication technology.
  • Include a data flow diagram.
  • Explain how the system works.
  • Explain the benefits and limitations.

Prepare a 5-minute presentation.

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Designer: lays out the poster or slides.
  • Researcher: chooses hardware/software/communication.
  • Illustrator: draws the data flow diagram.
  • Presenter: prepares and delivers the 5-minute talk.
  • Timekeeper: keeps the group on schedule.
👩‍🏫 Tip: Remind students that the poster should clearly show how the three components (hardware, software, communication) work together, not just list them separately.

10.5 Presenting Your Project ⏱️ 85–95 min

Your presentation should include:

  1. Problem and why it matters.
  2. Hardware components.
  3. Software components.
  4. Communication technology.
  5. Data flow.
  6. How the system solves the problem.
  7. What you learned.
🗣️ Peer Feedback:

After each presentation, give one star and one wish.

10.7 Reflection ⏱️ 97–100 min

After your presentation, write a reflection:

  • What was the hardest part?
  • How did understanding computer systems help you design your solution?
  • What would you improve?

🎟️ Exit Ticket — Complete before you leave

Write one paragraph answering:

“How did understanding computer systems help me design a solution?”

🦁 “A complete system is a work of art — every part in its place.”

10.8 Chapter Summary

  • A complete system uses hardware, software, and communication technology.
  • A data flow diagram shows how data moves through the system.
  • Presenting helps you explain your design to others.
  • Reflection helps you learn from the experience.
