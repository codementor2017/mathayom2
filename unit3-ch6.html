6.1 How Do You Communicate? ⏱️ 0–10 min

How do you communicate with friends and family?

Phone calls, text messages, LINE, email, video calls, social media.

How did people communicate 50 years ago?

Letters, landline phones, telegraph, face-to-face meetings.

Communication technology has changed dramatically.

🗣️ Think–Pair–Share (2 minutes):

What communication technology do you use most? Could you live without it for a week?

6.2 What is Communication Technology? ⏱️ 10–20 min

Communication technology is any tool or system that helps people send and receive information.

The basic communication model has five parts:

📤 Sender

The person or device sending the message.

📡 Transmitter

The device that sends the signal (phone, radio tower).

🌐 Channel

The medium the signal travels through (wire, air, fiber optic).

📥 Receiver

The device that receives the signal.

💬 Message

The information being sent.

6.3 A Timeline of Communication Technology ⏱️ 20–30 min

  • 1800s: Telegraph, telephone.
  • 1900s: Radio, television, fax.
  • 1990s: Email, SMS, early internet.
  • 2000s: Smartphones, social media, video calls.
  • 2010s–now: 5G, cloud communication, AI assistants.

Each invention made communication faster and easier.

6.4 Communication Then and Now ⏱️ 30–50 min

💬 Sending a message to a friend

Then: Letter by post (days or weeks).

Now: Text message or LINE (seconds).

Change: The internet and mobile phones.

📸 Sharing a photo

Then: Print and mail.

Now: Social media, messaging apps.

Change: Digital cameras and smartphones.

🎥 Attending a meeting

Then: Travel to a location.

Now: Video call.

Change: Internet and video conferencing software.

📰 Watching the news

Then: Newspaper or TV at fixed times.

Now: Online news, streaming, social media.

Change: The internet.

❓ Asking a question to a teacher

Then: In person, during class.

Now: Email, chat, or online platform.

Change: Internet and school platforms.

🗣️ Quick Check (2 minutes):

Which of these changes do you think has made the biggest difference in your daily life?

6.5 Group Activity: Design a Communication System ⏱️ 60–85 min

Work with a partner. Choose a scenario:

  • A school needs a system to notify parents about emergencies.
  • A hospital needs a system for doctors to communicate.
  • A village needs a system to share weather warnings.
  • A sports team needs a system to coordinate during games.
  • A family needs a system to stay in touch across countries.

Design a communication system:

  1. Sender, transmitter, channel, receiver, message.
  2. What technology would you use?
  3. What are the advantages and limitations?

👥 Suggested Group Roles

  • Designer: maps the five parts of the model.
  • Analyst: identifies technology and trade-offs.
  • Writer: documents the design.
  • Presenter: presents the system to the class.

6.5b Sharing & Presentations ⏱️ 85–95 min
